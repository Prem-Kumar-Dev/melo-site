Melo

An EPUB reader for iPhone and iPad

As you read, Melo scores the emotional tone of each paragraph on your device and gently shifts the background to match. Import your own DRM-free EPUB files, adjust type and themes, bookmark passages, and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, ask for a short on-device explanation of a passage.

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
